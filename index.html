My Journey

A Connection That Began
With a Dream

My connection to Spirit began over 35 years ago with a vivid dream of my Grandad, exactly one year after his passing. That single moment blossomed into a profound and humbling journey that has shaped my entire life.

I remain deeply grateful to Spirit for the opportunity to serve as a bridge between worlds. With integrity, love and deep respect, I connect with your loved ones — allowing you to experience their enduring love and support whilst offering compelling evidence of life beyond life.

I regularly serve Spiritual Churches and Centres across Essex, Kent, Suffolk and London, and I feel truly honoured every time I step onto the platform.

🐾 In loving memory of Mojo, my beloved Westie, who joined me at churches and centres for many years. He was welcomed everywhere with open arms and brought so much joy to so many people. Forever by my side in spirit.

"Where love is concerned, there is no distance too great"

What I Offer

Services

Whether you're seeking a private connection with a loved one or a shared experience with friends, I offer a range of ways to connect with Spirit.

✦

1-2-1 Readings

A personal, private sitting either at your home or mine. This is a gentle, intimate experience where I connect with your loved ones in Spirit, bringing through messages of love, evidence and guidance. Each reading is unique to you.

✦

Psychic Parties

Gather your friends and family for an unforgettable evening. I come to your home and provide individual card readings for each guest, with Spirit connections woven throughout. A wonderful way to share the experience together.

✦

Private Platform Events

An intimate evening of mediumship for a small group, held in the comfort of your home or a venue of your choice. I work from the platform, delivering messages from Spirit to your guests — an uplifting and deeply moving experience.

✦

Email Readings

Can't meet in person? Send me your question and I'll connect with Spirit on your behalf, providing a detailed written reading delivered straight to your inbox. A convenient way to receive guidance wherever you are.

✦

Workshops

Explore and develop your own spiritual gifts through a range of workshops. From meditation and healing to developing your mediumship, these sessions offer a supportive and nurturing space to grow on your spiritual journey.
